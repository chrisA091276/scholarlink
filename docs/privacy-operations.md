# Privacy and data handling

The software supports restricted access and configurable information collection. It does not establish the institution's legal basis, official policy or retention schedule. The operator must approve these before processing real student records.

- Publish the responsible institution and privacy officer's verified contact, purposes, information categories, recipients/service providers, lawful basis, student rights, retention periods, and complaint channel in the privacy notice. The shipped notice expressly identifies the policy details the institution must complete.
- Collect family/financial values only where a program's configured rules or application template requires them. Do not include household income or document contents in emails. Do not add income questions to every student profile.
- Students may see their own records; staff access student applications, documents, awards, and related reports within their assignments; administrators manage decisions and programs. Revoke inactive staff accounts promptly and review assignments periodically.
- Submission locks application answers and related profile edits. Request an explicit correction with remarks to reopen permitted editing. Document replacements preserve the earlier file and review trail privately. The first submission timestamp is retained across corrections.
- Access/correction/export requests must go through an identified authorized officer. Verify identity through established institutional channels, record the requester, scope, approving officer and delivery method, then provide only the requester's authorized records. Do not provide broad CSV exports to a student.
- Adopt explicit retention periods for rejected applications, successful awards, replaced documents, security logs, and backups. Run an authorized retention review that accounts for appeals, audits and legal holds before deletion. This project deliberately has no unattended destructive purge with an invented retention period. Document authorized deletions, backup expiry and access revocation.
- Test encrypted database and private-file backups and restoration; avoid exporting personal information into tickets, source control or public storage. Audit exports and decisions. CSV cells are escaped against spreadsheet formula injection.
- In an incident, restrict compromised accounts/keys, preserve relevant audit evidence, assess affected data, and follow the institution's incident and notification procedures. Production logging must not capture credentials, bearer tokens or full financial request bodies.

Design reference: the [Data Privacy Act of 2012](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/) and its [implementing rules](https://privacy.gov.ph/implementing-rules-regulations-data-privacy-act-2012/) describe transparency, legitimate purpose, proportionality, access safeguards and purpose-bound retention. Institutional legal/privacy review is still needed for the deployed policy.
